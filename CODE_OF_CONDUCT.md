# Code of conduct

This project is open to everyone who takes part in good faith, whatever their
background or experience. We expect the same from all of us, in issues, pull
requests and any other space of the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the
  project or to the subject.
- Give feedback on the work and not on the person, and take feedback the same
  way.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear before deciding what
  it meant.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or discriminatory attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or an
  identifier, without their permission.
- Sexualized language or attention, and anything else that a reasonable
  person would find hostile.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that go
against this code, and may block people who keep doing so. To report a
problem, write to lucas.bulgarelli@hotmail.com. Reports are read in private,
and the person who reports is not named to anyone else without their consent.
